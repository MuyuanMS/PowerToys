REMOVE HYPHENS / BODY TO PASTE INTO THE LIVE TEMPLATE

param([string]$Text)

[Console]::OutputEncoding = [Text.UTF8Encoding]::new($false)

$clean = $Text -replace '[-\u00AD\u2010\u2011]', ''
[Console]::Write($clean)

This removes ordinary, soft and Unicode hyphens. En/em dashes are preserved.
